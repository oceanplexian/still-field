STILL is a little experiment in light and motion. Thousands of points, a few waves, and no particular place to go.

Move your pointer to shift the perspective. Tap or click the open field to send a ripple through it. Try Wave, Orbit, or Drift to find your rhythm.

Built with a little math and native WebGL. No frameworks, no tracking, no noise. Your device’s reduced-motion preference is respected.
